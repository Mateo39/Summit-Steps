SUMMIT STEPS - putting it on the iPad
=====================================

The files in this folder are the whole app:
  index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and the icons folder.
Upload them together (keep the icons folder as a folder).

1. Put it online (once, about 10 minutes, easiest on a computer)

   Option A - GitHub Pages (free, stays up)
   a) Sign in at github.com (or create a free account).
   b) Click "+" > "New repository". Name it summit-steps, choose Public,
      tick "Add a README file", then "Create repository".
   c) Click "Add file" > "Upload files". Drag in everything from this folder
      (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, SETUP.txt and the icons folder).
      Click "Commit changes".
   d) Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment" choose
      "Deploy from a branch", branch "main", folder "/ (root)", then Save.
   e) After a minute or two the page shows your address, for example
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/summit-steps/

   Option B - Netlify Drop
   Go to app.netlify.com/drop, drag this whole folder onto the page,
   and create a free account when asked so the site is kept.

2. Install it on the iPad
   a) Open the address in Safari.
   b) Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then "Add to Home Screen",
      then "Add".
   c) Open Summit Steps from the new icon once while connected to Wi-Fi.
      After that it works offline.

3. Keep her progress safe
   Progress is stored on the iPad inside the app. Deleting the app's
   icon deletes its progress. Use Progress > "Save a backup" every week or two
   and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. "Restore from a backup" brings
   it back. The app reminds you if it has been 14 days.

4. Updates
   To update, upload the new index.html and sw.js over the old ones
   ("Add file" > "Upload files" again). The iPad picks up the new version
   the next time the app is opened online (sometimes it needs opening twice).

Notes
- The web address is public, but the app holds no personal information.
  Her progress stays on the iPad and is never uploaded.
- Progress in the installed app is separate from the Claude link version.
